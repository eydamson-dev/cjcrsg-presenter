Drawer evidence measurements
============================

Capture method: real controller window, GDK_BACKEND=x11, scripts/capture_window.sh.
The app has a 27 px native menu bar inside its X11 client window. Every image in
this folder is cropped to the WebView content box below that bar; the content-box
size is given per image below. No image contains the native menu bar.

Default and maximum geometry
----------------------------
4 px accent-tinted grab bar: rows 500..503 in the default Media image at x=500,
colour (145,15,96). Strip background below it (30,34,43); 4 + 36 = 40 px.
Default: drawer top y=500, content starts y=540, height 300, content 260.
Info column: left border x=990; spans x=990..1279 = 290 px.
Clock time glyphs: accent #f0008c, bbox x=1064..1200, y=644..669, centred in
the 290 px column.

Maximum: the page measures `.topbar` (56 px in the steady layout), sets
`--topbar-h`, and reports the same value to Rust. CSS uses
`max-height: calc(100vh - var(--topbar-h))`; the drag and Rust clamps use the
same measurement. At innerHeight 800 the maximum is 744; the max image starts
at y=56 and ends at y=799, so the whole drawer is visible. The workspace inner
frame lets the outer flex item shrink to zero instead of pushing the drawer off
the bottom.

Drag hit-test and grab offset
-----------------------------
`drag-hit-test-probe.mjs` loads the shipped `main.css` and `DRAWER_SCRIPT` in
headless Chromium and uses real Playwright mouse input (not a synthetic pointer
dispatched at a chosen element). `drag-hit-test-report.json` records:

- at 1280x800, 1024x640 and 400x600, a press at the bar centre hits
  `BUTTON.drawer-tab`, and dragging it (and a press at x=40 over a tab) sends a
  `drawer_resize`: height 502/342/302 respectively. The empty strip also
  resizes. This is the D1 fix.
- a press/release on the tab with no movement fires its click (count 1); a
  press with a pointermove suppresses the click (count 0).
- grabbing the strip 2 px below its top and moving the pointer 2 px up changes
  the drawer from 300 to 302 and leaves the pointer exactly 2 px below the new
  top at every viewport; the offset is preserved (D5).

against a 1246 maximum; the 800 captures show the window maximum. The 1400
value is not asserted.

Content overflow
----------------
drawer-overflow-1280x800.png: 45-asset project on Media at the default 300 px.
A vertical scrollbar is visible at x=983, rows are clipped at the drawer
boundary, the drawer stays at 300 (top y=500), and the 290 px clock column is
not pushed.

Clock
-----
Two captures of the same session 1.4 s apart read 16:29:18 and 16:29:19
(clock-tick.png).

Keyboard
--------
parity/tools/check_keys.mjs run against apps/desktop/assets/keys.js in headless
Chromium: 29/29 cases pass (keys-report.json). Ctrl+1..8 forwarded; Ctrl+0,
Ctrl+9, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+T not forwarded; Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2 ignored in an input.
