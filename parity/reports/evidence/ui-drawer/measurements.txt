Drawer evidence measurements
============================

Capture: real controller window, GDK_BACKEND=x11, scripts/capture_window.sh.
X11 client window: 1280x827. Native menu bar inside the window: 27 px.
WebView content box measured by window.innerWidth x window.innerHeight: 1280x800.
Content crops in this folder are the WebView area only: 1280x800.

Maximum derived from the shell
------------------------------
The page measures `.topbar` itself; in the steady layout it is 56 px
(44 px bar + 6 px padding top/bottom). `--topbar-h` is set to that measurement,
and CSS uses `max-height: calc(100vh - var(--topbar-h))`. The drag script and
the Rust clamp use the same number. At innerHeight 800 the maximum is 744.

drawer-max-1280x800.png: the drawer top bar starts at y=56 and the bottom row of
y=799 is still drawer panel (36,40,50): the full 744 px drawer is visible, with
its bottom edge on the window edge. Content height = 744 - 40 = 704.

The workspace is wrapped in an inner frame so its 12 px padding cannot force the
drawer past the window bottom; at the maximum the three columns have no height.

Content overflow
----------------
drawer-overflow-1280x800.png: a 45-asset project on the Media tab at the default
300 px height. The content area is 260 px, a vertical scrollbar is visible at
x=983, rows are clipped at the drawer boundary, and the drawer stays at 300
(top y=500). The 290 px clock column is not pushed sideways.

Session clamp and remembered heights
------------------------------------
Persistence (session.json observed):
  drag release at 520 -> layout.drawer_height = 520
  drag release at 79  -> collapsed to 40; remembered stayed 300 (sub-80 release
                         is not persisted)
  drag release at 80  -> open at 80; layout.drawer_height = 80
  remembered 80 at launch -> drawer opened at 300 (B5 floor); session stayed 80
  drag to the maximum at innerHeight 800 -> layout.drawer_height = 744
  hand-edited 1201 at innerHeight 800 -> rendered at the 744 maximum
  hand-edited 1201 at innerHeight 1302 -> session clamp wins: drawer 1200,
    top y=102, bottom y=1301 (drawer-clamp-1200-1280x1302.png)

Narrow width
------------
The window's minimum inner size is 1024x640 (main.rs), so 400x600 could not be
reached; at 1024x640 all eight labels are visible and the strip does not wrap
(drawer-narrow-1024x640.png).

Keyboard
--------
parity/tools/check_keys.mjs run against apps/desktop/assets/keys.js in headless
Chromium: 29/29 cases pass (keys-report.json). Ctrl+1..8 forwarded; Ctrl+0,
Ctrl+9, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+T not forwarded; Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2 ignored in an input.
