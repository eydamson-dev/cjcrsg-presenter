## Previous defects

### D1 — fixed

I re-ran the independent probe against the **current** `apps/desktop/assets/main.css`
and the current `DRAWER_SCRIPT` extracted from `apps/desktop/src/drawer.rs`.

At 1280×800:

- `document.elementFromPoint(640, 502)` is `BUTTON.drawer-tab` (the bar centre
  is over a tab, as before);
- a real mouse drag from (640,502) sends
  `drawer_resize {height: 502, inner_height: 800, top_bar: 56}`;
- a drag from x=40 over the active tab also sends a resize;
- a **click with no pointermove** at (640,502) produces one tab click (index 7;
  at 1024 it is index 5, at 400 index 2) and **no** `drawer_resize`;
- a click with no movement over the active tab at x=40 selects index 0;
- a drag suppresses the tab click (count 0).

At 1024×640 and 400×600 the same pattern holds: the bar centre hits a button,
the drag sends `drawer_resize` (342 and 302 in the probe), and the no-move click
selects a tab. The implementation removed the `button` exclusion from the
pointerdown handler; the click is suppressed only when a pointermove set
`moved`. This is the requested D1 behaviour.

### D5 — fixed

`DRAWER_SCRIPT` computes
`offsetY = round(innerHeight) - height - startY` at pointerdown and
`height = round(innerHeight) - ev.clientY - offsetY` on each move. `moved` is
set by any pointermove. A new unit test
`drag_height_keeps_the_pointer_at_its_grab_offset` mirrors the formula.

I also checked the actual CSS/JS: grabbing at the bar centre (2 px below the
top) and moving 2 px up leaves the drawer at height 302, top 498, with the
pointer at y=500 — the pointer stays 2 px below the top at 1280, 1024 and 400.
The previous "teleport to the pointer" behaviour is gone.
